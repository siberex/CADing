# Tripod Hook 1/4" Attachment

Fixtures to attach to the camera tripods with 1/4" mounts underneath the main column.

My mini-tripod have this attachment hole and I want to make a ring to attach a heavy steel carabiner to it, to increase tripod stability.

Also useful to hang tripod for storage or whatever.

Two models are provided: hook and ring (for extra rigidity). Ring could be used with carabiners or foot-loop.

# Recommended parameters

Materials:

Hook: PLA or stiffer (PETG don’t have enough rigidity for this thickness).

Ring: PLA or PETG.

Recommended step size: 0.1mm

Recommended nozzle size: 0.2mm

0.4mm will work too, threading is perfectly usable at 0.4mm vertical resolution.
